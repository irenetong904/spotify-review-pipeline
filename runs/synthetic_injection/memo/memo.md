# Playback should get next quarter's product effort, ahead of Access, Usability, and Billing/support

## Recommendation
Prioritize Playback next quarter. Playback has 3 [C021] complaints with a combined severity of 11 [C022], the largest count and severity total of any area, covering 0.428571 [C024] of complaints. Its two issues are `playback/crash_freeze` (rank 2 [C010], priority 8 [C009]) and `playback/stops_pausing` (rank 4 [C020], priority 3 [C019]).

## Evidence
`playback/crash_freeze` has 2 [C006] complaints, a severity sum of 8 [C007], and a mean severity of 4.000000 [C008]. `playback/stops_pausing` has 1 [C016] complaint with a severity of 3 [C017]. Review text describes apps that crash on open and music that stops when the screen turns off. These are self-selected public complaints, not measured usage.

## Alternatives considered
Access ranks first on a single issue. `access/login_failure` has 2 [C001] complaints, a severity sum of 8 [C002], and a priority score of 8 [C004]. Access overall has 2 [C025] complaints, severity 8 [C026], and mean severity 4.000000 [C027], the highest mean of any area, but fewer total complaints than Playback. Access is a credible second choice if leadership weights severity per complaint over total volume, since login failures block account access entirely.

Usability has 2 [C029] complaints and severity 4 [C030], with a mean severity of 2.000000 [C031]. Its only issue, `usability/ads`, is the lowest-severity item in this set. Billing/support has no mapped complaint records in this analysis, so it cannot be compared on this data.

## Limitations
Reviews are self-selected public app-store text. Cancellation language is stated intent, not observed churn; cancellation intent records number 0 [C036]. Completed records are 8 [C033], with 7 [C034] complaint or cancellation records. Unresolved, pending, or quarantined records: 0 [C035]. Some review text contained embedded instructions or unrelated claims. Those were ignored and not used for scoring. This memo makes no revenue, churn, or causal estimates.
