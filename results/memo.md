# Prioritize Usability next quarter while watching Billing/support growth

## Recommendation
Usability should get next quarter's main product effort. It ranks first on severity sum at 215962 [C055], ahead of Playback at 143019 [C062], Billing/support at 128973 [C069], and Access at 44570 [C076]. It also has the largest complaint count among the four named areas at 82988 [C054], a share of complaints of 0.279780 [C057]. Its mean severity of 2.602328 [C056] ranks fourth (4 [C052]), so it is a volume-driven priority rather than a severity-intensity one.

## Evidence
Within Usability, `usability/ads` is the highest-ranked issue at third overall (3 [C015]), with complaints of 28991 [C011] and severity sum of 66771 [C012]. Reviewer text includes `92bd7ff4-4322-4406-9033-f13e3079aa74`, which cites too many ads. `usability/general` ranks fifth overall (5 [C025]) with severity sum 49396 [C022]; see `9b42b7f8-2477-4516-b2de-6d46831fbad9` on basic features being limited. `usability/ui_layout` ranks sixth (6 [C030]) and `usability/queue_playlist` ranks seventh (7 [C035]); see `e7747485-42c5-4bb9-b1e2-1b4044eedb5a` on updates getting worse and `5185fc7e-8e72-45e4-8d85-91ab76c43fae` on liked songs not displaying.

## Recent trend
Usability complaints rose from 17976 [C091] in the earlier period to 32526 [C100] in the later period, and Usability has the largest count of the four named areas in the later period. Billing/support grew most among the four areas, from 3788 [C088] to 30084 [C097]. Access rose from 2110 [C087] to 5460 [C096], and Playback was roughly flat, from 12432 [C090] to 12506 [C099]. The Outside the four areas group rose from 8359 [C089] to 80852 [C098]. Overall complaint share of classified records rose from 0.301064 [C085] to 0.590511 [C094]. These changes are observed, not caused. The recommendation still holds because Usability leads the later period, but Billing/support growth should be monitored closely.

## Alternatives considered
Billing/support ranks third on severity sum (3 [C065]) and second on complaint count (2 [C067]) with 45504 [C068] complaints. Its top issue, `billing/subscription_entitlement`, ranks second overall (2 [C010]); see `c917e047-2d4b-41c5-ad41-bc9355b6d6e4` on paying for premium access. Its mean severity (2.834322 [C070]) ranks third (3 [C066]), so it is a credible second priority. Playback ranks second on severity sum (2 [C058]) and second on mean severity (2 [C059]), with mean 3.375797 [C063]. Its top issue `playback/general` ranks fourth (4 [C020]); see `ec296b31-61cb-4588-ba80-e8ab992b2281` on the app not staying active. Playback complaint volume is third (3 [C060]) and was flat across periods. Access ranks first on mean severity (1 [C073], mean 3.890877 [C077]), but fourth on severity sum (4 [C072]) and fourth on complaint count (4 [C074], 11455 [C075] complaints). Its issue `access/login_failure` is eighth overall (8 [C040]); see `97be5642-eab6-45f4-9c08-3aa28cc8e8ac` on login failures. Access is best treated as a targeted fix rather than the lead effort.

## Limitations
Reviews are self-selected public app-store text. Cancellation language is stated intent, not observed churn; cancellation-intent records were 2789 [C086] in the earlier period and 16003 [C095] in the later period, and 26366 [C105] overall. Quarantined records with empty review text total 13 [C103] and were excluded. Unclassified records pending or failed total 0 [C104]. Completed records total 660609 [C101], of which 296619 [C102] are complaint or cancellation records. Some issues, including shuffle and playback stopping, have no sampled review in this memo and are not discussed. No revenue, churn, or causal effects are estimated.
